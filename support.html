Support Center

We're here to help — submit a ticket and we'll reach out.

How it works: Submit this form to create a support ticket. If you configure a Discord webhook in your browser (localStorage), the form will also post details to that webhook.
Discord webhook: To enable Discord notifications, open your browser console and run: 
localStorage.setItem('discordWebhookUrl','https://discord.com/api/webhooks/XXX/YYY')
(Replace with your webhook URL.)

Frequently asked questions

How long will it take to get a reply?

We aim to respond within 24 business hours. Urgent issues are prioritized.

Can I continue on Discord?

Yes — if a Discord channel is created we will continue the conversation there. Otherwise we will reply via email.

Is my information secure?

Yes. We don't share your personal data with third parties and follow standard security practices.
