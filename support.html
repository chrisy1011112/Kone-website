Support Center

We're here to help! Submit your support request and we'll create a dedicated channel to assist you.

📞 How It Works

Submit your support request below and our Discord bot will automatically create a private support channel for you. You'll receive a notification with your channel details, and our support team will get back to you shortly.

💡 Note: To enable Discord notifications, configure your Discord webhook URL. The bot will create a private support channel and post your request there automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How long does it take to get a response?

We typically respond to support tickets within 24 hours during business days. Urgent issues may be prioritized and handled faster. You'll receive email notifications as your ticket progresses.

❓ Can I update my support ticket?

Yes! Once your support channel is created on Discord, you can continue the conversation there. Alternatively, reply to any email notifications you receive.

❓ What if I don't use Discord?

No problem! We'll handle all communication via email. You'll receive a ticket number for reference, and we'll keep you updated via email throughout the support process.

❓ Is my information secure?

Absolutely. All support conversations are encrypted and stored securely. We never share your personal information with third parties and comply with all data protection regulations.

❓ Can I track my ticket status?

Yes! You'll receive a unique ticket ID when you submit your request. Use this ID to track your ticket status on our support portal or in your Discord channel.

❓ What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for larger subscriptions. Contact billing support for custom arrangements.
